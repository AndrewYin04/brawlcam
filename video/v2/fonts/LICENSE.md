# Fonts

The game UI's brand fonts, downloaded from Google Fonts (latin subset) so renders are deterministic and work offline.

- **Bungee** (David Jonathan Ross): SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bungee. `web/src/style.css` loads it for the logo, banners and HUD.
- **Inter** (variable, latin subset; Rasmus Andersson): SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter. The v1 video cards used it for body text.

The OFL allows bundling and redistributing the fonts with other software. It does not allow selling them on their own.
