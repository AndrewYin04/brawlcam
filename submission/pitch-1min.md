# Throw Hands: 1-minute spoken pitch (HackTX26 Best Pitch)

Spoken only: no slides, no demo. About 145 words, under 60 seconds at a natural pace. **[BEAT]** marks the one physical move (no setup needed).

## 60-second version

**Hook (~8 s).** What if the only controller you needed was your own fist? **[BEAT: throw a real right hook in the air]** That just landed. In our game, it really does.

**Problem (~10 s).** The best game nights are loud, with friends and trash talk. But motion games need a console, an accessory, and everyone in the same room.

**Solution (~15 s).** Throw Hands runs in your browser with just a webcam. Open a link, stand up, and fight. Your fighter copies your punches, your guard and your footwork, in a 3D octagon, against a friend on another laptop or phone, or up to eight players at once.

**What makes it different (~17 s).** A pose model running in your browser is the controller. Cross your arms for a super shield, throw a big uppercut to launch your opponent into the cage, and a live AI commentator roasts every whiff.

**Close (~8 s).** No download, no account, no controller. Your body is the controller. Fight your friends at throwhands.tech.
